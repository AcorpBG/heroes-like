# Authored scenarios and campaigns are obsolete

Owner decision, 2026-10-05: the authored scenarios in `scenarios.json` and the campaigns in `campaigns.json` are obsolete and will be redone completely. Put no effort into them before that rework: no new scenarios or campaigns, fixes, retuning or validation work on them. Leave the data unchanged; the game still loads it.

The other content files here are shared definitions that generated maps use, and they stay live. See `docs/authored-scenarios-obsolete.md` for the full scope and the list of obsolete tests and validators.
