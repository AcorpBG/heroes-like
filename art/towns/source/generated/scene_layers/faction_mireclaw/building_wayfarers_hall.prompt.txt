Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original transparent raster building layer for a painted fantasy strategy town.
Primary request: paint one complete Mireclaw Wayfarers Hall, a hired-captains meeting lodge adapted to a marsh ferry town. A long, low dark-timber hall has a heavy weathered reed roof, a recessed open gathering porch, hanging travel cloaks, a few rolled bedrolls and an unlettered notice board beneath the eaves. One small roof vent and a green-yellow lantern distinguish the entrance. A short timber approach at the front-left connects to the existing town's shoreline; short pilings and rope rails belong to the building.
Scene context, described from the project's inspected original Mireclaw village: drowned timber causeways, blackwater and low reed dwellings at a murky golden dusk. This lodge will stand on the middle-distance right bank behind the foreground communal fire platform. It must read as part of the established settlement, not a freestanding diorama.
Style/medium: finely painted original fantasy environment art with practical, lived-in marsh materials; muted yellow-olive thatch, dark green-black wet wood, faded hides, dull bone details and rough rope. Warm backlight from upper-left, shadowed near facade with small restrained lantern warmth. No glossy miniature look, saturated purple trim or generic medieval stone inn.
Composition: horizontal three-quarter lodge viewed about 25 degrees above the ground, long front facade and left side visible. The front-left porch approach points toward the viewer, with modest roof height and a clean readable entrance. Show the complete roof, supports, stair and rails with transparent margin on all sides. Broad 3:2 composition.
Background: actual transparent alpha, including open spaces under the porch and between railings. Paint only the architecture and attached narrow approach; no separate soil/water base, grass island, backdrop, ground shadow rectangle, sky, trees, other buildings, people, checkerboard or scene fragment.
Constraints: original project artwork, no borrowed game pixels or protected designs, no lettering, logos, UI frame or watermark. Clean antialiased transparent RGBA cutout designed to join an unchanged painted panorama.
