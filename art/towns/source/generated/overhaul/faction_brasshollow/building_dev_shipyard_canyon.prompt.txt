Inside the masked area only, paint the town's boatworks clinging to the steep basalt cliff face below the edge of the flat terrace, above the slag river. Two heavy iron rails run diagonally down the cliff face as a slipway, from the cliff's top lip at the upper left of the masked area down into the glowing river at the lower right. On the rails an iron-hulled boat with a riveted black hull, a brass rubbing strake and a short brass funnel rests on a wheeled iron cradle, its bow pointing down the rails, held back by heavy chains that run up to a low iron winch drum bolted to the rock at the top of the rails. Dark dressed stone footings and iron brackets fix the rails to the rock, with a few lanterns of hot orange light. Everything is low and hangs on the cliff face: nothing stands on the terrace above, nothing rises above the cliff's top lip, and the flat terrace above stays empty bare rock. It fills the masked area, but no part of it touches the edges of the masked area.
