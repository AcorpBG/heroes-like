Image 1 is a detail of a painting of a wild basalt canyon at dusk with a glowing slag river, where the mining town of an industrial contract power is being built. Image 2 shows other buildings of this town, for their materials, colours and details only.

Inside the masked area only, paint the town's boatworks on the steep basalt cliff above the slag river. Two heavy iron rails run diagonally down the cliff face as a slipway, from the cliff edge at the upper left down toward the river at the lower right. On the rails an iron-hulled boat with a riveted black hull, a brass rubbing strake and a short brass funnel rests on a wheeled iron cradle held back by heavy chains, its bow pointing down the rails. At the top of the rails, on the cliff edge, stand a squat riveted iron gantry crane and a small soot-dark brick winch house with hot orange light in its narrow windows and a charcoal banner with a brass chevron. Stacked iron plates and coiled chain beside it. Everything is bolted to the rock on dark dressed stone footings. It fills the masked area, but no part of it touches the edges of the masked area.

Materials and mood: heavy, angular, hot and audited: riveted black iron, dark dressed stone, soot-dark brick, brass trim, hot orange light in narrow windows, charcoal banners with a brass chevron. Not dwarven, not a castle, not decorative steampunk.
