Layout, from image 1: a sea channel runs up the left side of the picture from a harbor basin in the lower left, past a tall sea stack, to the mouth of the fjord at the upper left, where the low sun sets over the open sea. Across the mouth, two squat towers on rocks hold a great chain boom. On the sea stack stands the bell hall, a tall dark hall with a great open belfry of bronze bells, the focal point, joined to the shore by an arched stone bridge. To the right of the channel, the shore rises as a broad, rugged hillside of dark rock to cliffs along the top of the picture, split by a ravine with a thin stream that runs down to a small cove at the bottom middle. The town grows over this hillside: each building stands where its block is in image 1 and about as large, on its own ledge, spur or outcrop at its own height, joined by stairs cut into the rock and short paths. There are no rows, no terraces, no grid and no buildings other than these.

Buildings, by their place in image 1: the tall tower near the top is a lighthouse crowned with a ring of round mirrors; the small domed building to its left is a chart house; the long shed by the basin is a covered slipway where a ship with a silvered mirror hull is being built; the pale block with the round mark is a vault of salt-white stone with a heavy round door; the three small stalls are a market with awnings; the ring in the cove is a flooded sea pen walled with whale ribs, where a vast pale leviathan rests half-submerged; the tall narrow block at the top right is a watchtower. The others are halls, sheds, chapels and lodges of the same town in the style of image 2. Each building is turned at its own angle, showing two sides, and stays readable with a clear silhouette.

Camera: an elevated oblique view, about 30 degrees down, with strong perspective: buildings near the bottom of the picture are larger and clearer, those near the top smaller, hazier and bluer. Black-sailed funeral ships with pale lanterns lie moored in the basin and in the channel. Low fog lies on the water and in the ravine, but every building stays readable and the picture is not murky.

Light: the low, pale-gold sun at the upper left behind the chain towers puts a thin warm rim on roof edges and left-hand sides; walls facing the viewer are in cool blue-grey shade; windows and lanterns glow warm amber; a few mirrors glint white.

Materials: tarred timber, black slate roofs, salt-white stone, verdigris bronze, rope and chains, bronze bells, and the bone-white ribs of old ships reused as arches; weathered and restrained, not ornate. Style as image 2: rich hand-painted matte painting with visible brush texture, no outlines, no cel shading, no isometric game-icon look, not photoreal.
