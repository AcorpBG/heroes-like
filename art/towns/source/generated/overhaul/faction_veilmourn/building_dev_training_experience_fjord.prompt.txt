Image 1 is a detail of a painting of a wild fjord at blue dusk, with black-sailed ships moored in a sea channel below a rugged hillside of dark rock, where the harbor town of a fog-bound funeral fleet is being built. Image 2 is a detail from a painting of the finished town; use it only for the style, materials and painting technique, not for the layout, the design or the size.

Inside the masked area only, paint the hall of chronicles, where the memories of the drowned are kept: a tall narrow archive hall with a steep roof and a small bell turret on its ridge, sealed memory niches glowing faintly in a side arcade, a small outside stair to an upper reading alcove and a bronze-caged lamp over the door. It is turned about thirty degrees to the left, so its front and right side show. It stands on the rocky shoulder of the hillside below the lighthouse knoll, far away, on a low foundation of rough-cut salt-grey stone set into the uneven rock, with a short worn path or steps leading to it. It fills the masked area, but no part of it touches the edges of the masked area.

Materials and mood: the harbor of a fog-bound funeral fleet: tarred black ship timber, black slate roofs, salt-white and pale grey stone, verdigris bronze, rope and chains, the bone-white ribs of old ships reused as arches and braces, small round mirrors of smoky silver, warm amber light in the windows and lanterns. Weathered, quiet and restrained, not ornate; not a castle, not a cathedral.

Paint it matched to image 1: the same perspective and scale for that distance and loose painterly brushwork. The low sun is far away at the upper left, at the mouth of the fjord, so fronts facing the viewer are in cool blue-grey shade with a thin warm rim light on roof edges and left-hand sides, and a soft shadow falls toward the viewer and to the right. It is far away, so it sits in the misty blue haze of that distance, with softened contrast and lifted darks. It must look painted into the scene.

Keep the rocks, water, fog and sky around it as crisp and detailed as the rest of image 1; do not soften or blur them. No snow or frost. No text, lettering or numbers. No people and no animals.
