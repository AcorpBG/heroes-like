Image 1 is a detail of a painting of a wild fjord at blue dusk, with black-sailed ships moored in a sea channel below the cliff of a tall sea stack, where the harbor town of a fog-bound funeral fleet is being built. Image 2 shows other buildings of this town, for their materials, colours and details only.

Inside the masked area only, paint the town's shipyard: an open boatyard built out over the water on heavy timber piles at the foot of the sea stack's cliff. A low pile-built wooden platform with a short slipway running down into the water; a tall timber sheer-legs crane leaning over the water with ropes and a hanging block; a small black-sailed boat afloat alongside the platform, nearly finished, its mast up and its sail furled; stacks of planks, coiled rope and barrels on the platform; a small boatwright's hut with a black slate roof and one amber-lit window; two lanterns on posts. The boat lies open to the sky: no shed and no roof over it. Its long side faces the viewer, seen a little from above. It fills the masked area, but no part of it touches the edges of the masked area.

Materials and mood: the harbor of a fog-bound funeral fleet: tarred black ship timber, black slate roofs, salt-white and pale grey stone, verdigris bronze, rope and chains, the bone-white ribs of old ships reused as arches and braces, small round mirrors of smoky silver, warm amber light in the windows and lanterns. Weathered, quiet and restrained, not ornate; not a castle, not a cathedral.

Paint it matched to image 1: the same perspective and scale for that distance and loose painterly brushwork. The low sun is far away at the upper left, at the mouth of the fjord, so fronts facing the viewer are in cool blue-grey shade with a thin warm rim light on roof edges and left-hand sides, and a soft shadow falls toward the viewer and to the right. Its lanterns and window are reflected in the dark water below. It is near the viewer, clear and detailed. It must look painted into the scene.

Keep the rocks, water, fog and sky around it as crisp and detailed as the rest of image 1; do not soften or blur them. No snow or frost. No text, lettering or numbers. No people and no animals.
