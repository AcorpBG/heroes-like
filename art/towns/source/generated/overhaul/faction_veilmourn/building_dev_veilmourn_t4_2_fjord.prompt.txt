Image 1 is a detail of a painting of a wild fjord at blue dusk, with black-sailed ships moored in a sea channel below a rugged hillside of dark rock, where the harbor town of a fog-bound funeral fleet is being built. Image 2 shows the keel barracks of the Mournhull Sentinels, which this building later grows into; its size comes from the masked area, not from image 2.

Inside the masked area only, paint the crew lodge of the Gloamkeel Bulwarks, the earlier and smaller stage of the building in image 2: a stout compact lodge built from a massive cut ship keel, under one single low roof, with curved iron-bound keel shields leaned along its heavy walls and a low armoured door. It has no tower, no spire and no lantern on its roof; the prow tower of image 2 is not built yet. Use the same materials, colours and details as image 2. It is turned about thirty degrees to the left, so its long front and right end show. It stands on the rocky slope at the near right, in the foreground, on a low foundation of rough-cut salt-grey stone set into the uneven rock, with a short worn path or steps leading to it. It fills the masked area, but no part of it touches the edges of the masked area.

Materials and mood: the harbor of a fog-bound funeral fleet: tarred black ship timber, black slate roofs, salt-white and pale grey stone, verdigris bronze, rope and chains, the bone-white ribs of old ships reused as arches and braces, small round mirrors of smoky silver, warm amber light in the windows and lanterns. Weathered, quiet and restrained, not ornate; not a castle, not a cathedral.

Paint it matched to image 1: the same perspective and scale for that distance and loose painterly brushwork. The low sun is far away at the upper left, at the mouth of the fjord, so fronts facing the viewer are in cool blue-grey shade with a thin warm rim light on roof edges and left-hand sides, and a soft shadow falls toward the viewer and to the right. It is near the viewer, clear and detailed. It must look painted into the scene.

Keep the rocks, water, fog and sky around it as crisp and detailed as the rest of image 1; do not soften or blur them. No snow or frost. No text, lettering or numbers. No people and no animals.
