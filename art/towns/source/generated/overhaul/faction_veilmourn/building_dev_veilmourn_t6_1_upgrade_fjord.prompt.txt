Image 1 is a detail of a painting of a wild fjord at blue dusk, with black-sailed ships moored in a sea channel below a rugged hillside of dark rock, where the harbor town of a fog-bound funeral fleet is being built. Image 2 is a detail from a painting of the finished town; it shows the slipway of the Duskpass Revenants. Keep that building's design; its size comes from the masked area, not from image 2.

Inside the masked area only, paint the slipway of the Duskpass Revenants: a great covered slipway: an open-sided shed of tarred black timber with a steep slate roof on heavy posts and bone-white rib arches, sheltering on its slipway a black raiding ship whose hull is clad in silvered mirror plates, with lanterns hung under the roof; the whole shed fits inside the masked area. It is turned about thirty degrees to the right, so its long side and its open left end show. It stands at the water's edge at the foot of the hillside, behind the long wooden quay, with its open end facing the channel, on heavy timber posts and stone footings at the back of the quay. It fills the masked area, but no part of it touches the edges of the masked area.

Materials and mood: the harbor of a fog-bound funeral fleet: tarred black ship timber, black slate roofs, salt-white and pale grey stone, verdigris bronze, rope and chains, the bone-white ribs of old ships reused as arches and braces, small round mirrors of smoky silver, warm amber light in the windows and lanterns. Weathered, quiet and restrained, not ornate; not a castle, not a cathedral.

Paint it matched to image 1: the same perspective and scale for that distance and loose painterly brushwork. The low sun is far away at the upper left, at the mouth of the fjord, so fronts facing the viewer are in cool blue-grey shade with a thin warm rim light on roof edges and left-hand sides, and a soft shadow falls toward the viewer and to the right. It is near the viewer, clear and detailed. It must look painted into the scene.

Keep the rocks, water, fog and sky around it as crisp and detailed as the rest of image 1; do not soften or blur them. No snow or frost. No text, lettering or numbers. No people and no animals.
