Paint with a fine, smooth hand, crisp at full size: woven branches and glass panes clean and clear, moss as soft velvety masses, leaves and blossom as soft clumps with smoothly blended colour. No blocky or square dabs, no mosaic, no pixel-like texture, no fine parallel hatching.
