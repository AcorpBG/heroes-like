Image 1 is a detail of a painting of a vast wild swamp at a sickly gold-green dusk, with still black pools, reed beds, mossy hummocks and giant moss-hung cypresses in the mist, where the stilt town of predatory bog clans is being built. Image 2 is a detail from a painting of the finished town; it shows the great den of the Gorefen Packlords. Keep that design; its size comes from the masked area, not from image 2.

Inside the masked area only, paint the great den of the Gorefen Packlords: a low, broad den under a horned roof ribbed with giant curved bones, with bone-fenced beast pens in its open front, drying hides and a feeding fire, all under the same roof. The roof and its horns stay low, inside the masked area. It is turned about thirty degrees to the left, so its front and right side show. It stands on the reedy mud near the viewer, at the right, on packed mud, logs and stilts. It fills the masked area, but no part of it touches the edges of the masked area.

Materials and mood: the town of predatory bog clans who rule by ferry chains, drum law and the hunt: dark weathered timber bound with rope and rusted chain, steep shaggy roofs of thatch and black shingles, bone-white horns, antlers and tusks, hides, moss and reeds, green glass witch-lanterns, orange fire light in doorways. Savage, watchful and weathered, restrained, not cartoonish; not a castle, not a horror film.

Paint it matched to image 1: the same perspective and scale for that distance and loose painterly brushwork. The pale gold dusk glow is low and far away at the upper right, so fronts facing the viewer are in deep cool green shade with a thin gold rim on roof edges, horns and right-hand sides, lit from within by its own fires and lanterns, and a soft shadow falls toward the viewer and to the left. It is near the viewer, clear and detailed. It must look painted into the scene, readable against the dark swamp.

Keep the reeds, moss, mud, water, trees and mist around it as crisp and detailed as the rest of image 1; do not soften or blur them. No snow or frost. No smoke plumes. No text, lettering or numbers. Dark hunched shapes of beasts crouch in the shadow of the pens; no people.
