Image 1 is a detail of a painting of a vast wild swamp at a sickly gold-green dusk, with still black pools, reed beds, mossy hummocks and giant moss-hung cypresses in the mist, where the stilt town of predatory bog clans is being built. Image 2 shows the spore tower, which this building later grows into; its size comes from the masked area, not from image 2.

Inside the masked area only, paint the witch tower, the earlier and smaller stage of the building in image 2: a squat tower of lashed timber of two levels with a green lantern on top. Use the same materials, colours and details as image 2. It stands at the upper left, built around the great broken stump, far away across the swamp, in the mist, rooted in the stump and its roots. It fills the masked area, but no part of it touches the edges of the masked area.

Materials and mood: the town of predatory bog clans who rule by ferry chains, drum law and the hunt: dark weathered timber bound with rope and rusted chain, steep shaggy roofs of thatch and black shingles, bone-white horns, antlers and tusks, hides, moss and reeds, green glass witch-lanterns, orange fire light in doorways. Savage, watchful and weathered, restrained, not cartoonish; not a castle, not a horror film.

Paint it matched to image 1: the same perspective and scale for that distance and loose painterly brushwork. The pale gold dusk glow is low and far away at the upper right, so fronts facing the viewer are in deep cool green shade with a thin gold rim on roof edges, horns and right-hand sides, lit from within by its own fires and lanterns, and a soft shadow falls toward the viewer and to the left. It is in the middle distance, in a thin green mist. It must look painted into the scene, readable against the dark swamp.

Keep the reeds, moss, mud, water, trees and mist around it as crisp and detailed as the rest of image 1; do not soften or blur them. No snow or frost. No smoke plumes. No text, lettering or numbers. No people and no animals.
