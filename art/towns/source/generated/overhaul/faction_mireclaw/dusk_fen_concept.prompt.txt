Paint the town screen background of an original fantasy strategy game, in the spirit of classic hand-painted 1990s strategy-game town screens but entirely original: Duskfen, the home town of the Mireclaw Covenant, predatory bog clans that rule by ferry chains, drum law and the hunt of wounded prey. One coherent matte painting with dramatic depth and volume, at a sickly gold-green dusk.

The place is a swamp, not a river: there is no river, no channel and no flowing water anywhere. A vast drowned bog stretches away to the horizon: still black pools scummed with green duckweed and lily pads, beds of tall reeds and cattails, mud flats, mossy hummocks, tangled mangrove roots, rotting fallen trunks, and colossal ancient cypresses with trunks as thick as towers, hung with grey moss. Low mist lies in layers between the trees.

Camera and depth: seen from high in the crown of one of the colossal cypresses on the right-hand side, looking out across the swamp toward the upper right, about 30 degrees down, with strong perspective and great height. Nothing lies on one level: hummocks, root mounds as tall as houses, leaning dead giants and platforms built up the trunks stand at many different heights, with dark water and shadow between them. Far away, under the pale gold dusk glow, the swamp fades into mist behind a palisade gate of horned stakes, small and hazy, and layer after layer of giant cypress silhouettes. Buildings near the bottom of the picture are large, crisp and saturated, and those at the frame edges may be partly cut off; buildings further off get much smaller, paler and hazier, some only dark silhouettes in the gold-green mist. Where one stands in front of another it overlaps it.

Each building stands where its block is in image 1, on its own hummock, root mound or crag, or on stilts driven into the mud and the pools, joined by crooked plank walks, rope bridges and chains. The town looks grown, not planned: no rows, no terraces, no grid, no flat plain, no even spacing, and no buildings other than these.

Buildings, by their place in image 1: the large hall on the high root mound left of the middle is the drum hall, a great hall of dark thorned timber with a steep roof crowned by huge curved horns and great hide drums under its eaves, the focal point; the tall tower at the upper left is a spore tower of twisted timber with a cage of glowing green witch-light at its top, on a giant stump; the three stalls at the left are a market on stilts with hide awnings; the two posts over the pool in the middle hold a heavy rusted chain across it; on the low mossy island in the mere, on dry ground, stands a ring of tall bone stakes and chains, and inside it a colossal bog beast like a giant elk with a crown of great antlers lies on the moss, entirely on land and out of the water; the dock at the bottom, on the near shore of the mere, is the ferry yard, a slip of black timber with a great chain winch and a flat ferry hull on the stocks, large in the foreground; the large block at the right edge is a den of bone-fenced beast pens under a horned roof, the largest building in the foreground. The others are halls, lodges, dens, sheds and shrines of the same town, each different in shape, with steep thatch and shingle roofs, horns, thorns and chains. Each building is turned at its own angle, showing two sides, with a clear silhouette.

Light: the pale gold dusk glow low at the upper right fills the far swamp with gold-green haze and lights the mist between the trees; the near hummocks, roots and trunks are in deep cool green-black shadow with a thin gold rim along roof edges, horns and root tops, so large shapes of light and shadow carry the picture. Green witch-lanterns, orange drum fires and lit doorways glow in the dark; the pools shine pale gold in the distance and lie black nearby with long reflections.

Frame: the top 12% is dusk sky and far forest. The lower right corner is dark foreground roots and bark.

Materials: dark weathered timber bound with rope and rusted chain, steep roofs of thatch and black shingles, bone-white horns, antlers and tusks, hides, moss, reeds, green glass witch-lanterns; savage, predatory and watchful, weathered and restrained, not cartoonish, not a horror film.

Style: rich hand-painted digital matte painting with visible brush texture and shapes that read at small size. No outlines, no cel shading, no isometric game-icon look, not a map or a diorama, not photoreal. No text, no lettering, no signature, no interface elements, no people in the foreground.
