Image 1 is a detail of a painting of a wild river gorge at golden sunset, where a river falls through a staircase of stone locks into a dark harbour basin between bare rocky slopes, and where the river town of a league of charter courts is being built. Image 2 shows the lantern tower, which this building later grows into; its size comes from the masked area, not from image 2.

Inside the masked area only, paint the lantern house, the earlier and smaller stage of the building in image 2: a squat stone tower of two storeys with a small brass lantern cage on top. Use the same materials, colours and details as image 2. It stands on the rocky shoulder at the foot of the tall rock pinnacle, high above the gorge, far away, on a low foundation of rough-cut warm grey stone set into the uneven rock, with a short worn path or steps leading to it. It fills the masked area, but no part of it touches the edges of the masked area.

Materials and mood: the river town of a league of charter courts that rules by lockworks, beacon fire and civic law: warm grey dressed stone, red clay tile roofs, dark oak timber, wrought iron, brass fire baskets, red banners with a gold flame, warm orange light in the windows and lanterns. Sturdy, civic and lawful, weathered and restrained, not ornate; not a fairy-tale castle, not a cathedral.

Paint it matched to image 1: the same perspective and scale for that distance and loose painterly brushwork. The low golden sun is far away at the upper left, at the far end of the gorge, so fronts facing the viewer are in soft cool shade with a warm rim light on roof edges and left-hand sides, and a soft shadow falls toward the viewer and to the right. It is far away, so it sits in the warm golden haze of that distance, with softened contrast and lifted darks. It must look painted into the scene.

Keep the rocks, scrub, pines, water and sky around it as crisp and detailed as the rest of image 1; do not soften or blur them. No snow or frost. No smoke plumes. No text, lettering or numbers. No people and no animals.
