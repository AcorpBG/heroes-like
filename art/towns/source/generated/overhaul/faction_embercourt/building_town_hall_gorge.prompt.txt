Inside the masked area only, paint the town's first council hall, the earlier and smaller stage of the building in image 2: a single modest hall of grey stone and dark oak framing under a red tile roof, with a small brass fire basket on an iron bracket over its heavy plank door and one small red banner. Use the same materials, colours and details as image 2. It faces the viewer, turned slightly to the right, so its front and a little of its left side show. It stands on the broad rock shelf at the top of the spur left of the locks, above the sheer drop to the basin, on a low foundation of rough-cut warm grey stone set into the uneven rock, with a short worn path or steps leading to it. It fills the masked area, but no part of it touches the edges of the masked area.

Materials and mood: the river town of a league of charter courts that rules by lockworks, beacon fire and civic law: warm grey dressed stone, red clay tile roofs, dark oak timber, wrought iron, brass fire baskets, red banners with a gold flame, warm orange light in the windows and lanterns. Sturdy, civic and lawful, weathered and restrained, not ornate; not a fairy-tale castle, not a cathedral.

Paint it matched to image 1: the same perspective and scale for that distance and loose painterly brushwork. The low golden sun is far away at the upper left, at the far end of the gorge, so fronts facing the viewer are in soft cool shade with a warm rim light on roof edges and left-hand sides, and a soft shadow falls toward the viewer and to the right. It is in the middle distance, in a light warm haze. It must look painted into the scene.

Keep the rocks, scrub, pines, water and sky around it as crisp and detailed as the rest of image 1; do not soften or blur them. No snow or frost. No smoke plumes. No text, lettering or numbers. No people and no animals.
