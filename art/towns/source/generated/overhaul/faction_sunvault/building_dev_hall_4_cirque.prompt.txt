Precise, luminous and disciplined, restrained, not ornate, not a fairy-tale palace, not cartoonish.

Paint it matched to image 1: the same perspective and scale for that distance and loose painterly brushwork. Keep the rock, snow, pines and water around it as crisp and detailed as the rest of image 1; do not soften or blur them. Crystals and glass glint, but there are no beams of light, no lens flares and no glow around the building. No smoke. No text, lettering or numbers. No people and no animals.
