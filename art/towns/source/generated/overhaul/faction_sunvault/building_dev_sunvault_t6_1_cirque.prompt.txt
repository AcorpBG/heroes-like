Image 1 is a detail of a painting of a high mountain cirque on a bright, cold, clear morning, with bare golden-grey rock shelves, patches of snow, dwarf pines, snow peaks and a turquoise glacial lake, where the town of the Sunvault Compact is being built. Image 2 shows the bastion foundry of the Corona Bulwarks, which this building later grows into; its size comes from the masked area, not from image 2.

Inside the masked area only, paint the hall of the Aurora Bastions, the earlier and smaller stage of the building in image 2: a smaller hall with one gilded gable with a glass skylight and an arched open bay in its front. Use the same materials, colours and details as image 2. It is turned about thirty degrees to the left, so its front and right side show. It stands on the broad rock terrace right of the middle, near the viewer, on a broad terrace of pale stone blocks. It fills the masked area, but no part of it touches the edges of the masked area.

Materials and mood: the town of crystal-array commanders who rule by solar crystals, lens relays and harmonic command: pale gold sandstone, white marble, gilded roofs and domes, brass instruments, glass, and clear prismatic crystal in blue, violet and rose. Precise, luminous and disciplined, restrained, not ornate, not a fairy-tale palace, not cartoonish.

Paint it matched to image 1: the same perspective and scale for that distance and loose painterly brushwork. The sun is high at the upper left, so roofs and left-hand faces are bright and warm, right-hand faces are in clear cool blue shade, and a crisp shadow falls to the right and toward the viewer. It is near the viewer, crisp, saturated and detailed. It must look painted into the scene, readable against the rock.

Keep the rock, snow, pines and water around it as crisp and detailed as the rest of image 1; do not soften or blur them. Crystals and glass glint, but there are no beams of light, no lens flares and no glow around the building. No smoke. No text, lettering or numbers. In the open bay stand two towering suits of white-and-gold plate armour, motionless; no people.
