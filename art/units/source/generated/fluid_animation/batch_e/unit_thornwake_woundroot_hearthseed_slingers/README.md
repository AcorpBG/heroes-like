# Hearthseed Slingers original action sources

A ranged adult forest woman with one forked sling staff in her right hand, twin straps and one leather ammunition pouch, and a small wicker buckler on her left arm. During a throw one strap releases while the fixed strap retains the pouch; the same pouch is reloaded and the release end reconnected. Existing eight-pose articulated idle and map idle are preserved byte-for-byte. Seven dedicated H3 actions use original anatomical and prop guides, fixed camera/scale and neutral gray plates. Support is a physical buckler acknowledgement; the actual ranged animation is a mechanical sling release and reload. Projectile travel belongs to the game.

The fallen guides use forearm/body ground contacts rather than the foreground loose staff as their anchors. The original seated guide placed the body above the common ground and both fallen paintings omitted the ready pose's two attached amber fork ornaments. Separate imagegen derivatives restore those ornaments, with original images and exact generation lineage preserved. Each new master is registered once at the original anatomical scale; generated frames keep a fixed extraction scale and anchor. The lower canvas margin retains the full fallen staff and pouch.

The first death take's overhand swing and equipment loss, the second take's hard kneel-to-seated cut, and the third take's duplicated fixed fork ornaments are rejected complete originals. The fourth recipe uses only the original ready and corrected resting endpoints for continuous lowering. The seated master remains preserved as unused source. Original generation, videos, latents, references, matte recipes and rejected takes are retained. Production acceptance requires personal chronological RGB/RGBA and enlarged action review, native and reflected review, and actual game action and map checks.

Run `run_bound_sources.py` with at most two takes. It keeps one continuous shared GPU lease through sampling, decoding, semantic matting and model release. Complete personal CPU review before another invocation. Temporary review pages are rebuildable and removed at completion.
