This source directory owns only `unit_veilmourn_saltbell_casters`. The original ivory hood, dark blue clothing, two human arms and legs, anatomical right-hand rope coil and tethered bronze bell are authoritative. The anatomical left hand is free. Belt ornaments remain original costume; they are not additional thrown weapons. Current content describes a ranged bell cast and Saltbell False Echo. Support uses a separate chest-level bell raise, supported by the free left palm, subtle ringing wrist and bowed hood, followed by recovery.

The inspected original idle poses 16–23 shows coherent palm and forearm articulation while the equipped hand and planted boots remain stable. Preserve its eight paintings and 240 ms timing exactly in battle and map playback. Seven dedicated actions are required: reciprocal walk, compact bent-left-elbow contact, recoil, held forearm guard, quiet support gesture, tethered horizontal bell casting stroke, and supported equipped collapse. Final acceptance requires full chronological original/matte, enlarged detail, native battle/map, reflected gallery and actual action-clock review.

The physical corrections use original identity art plus retained imagegen fist, elbow, chest-bell, halfway crouch and supported side-lean guides, with exact prompt/reference/alpha recipes. Failed gray-halo guide attempts remain source. Model, sampling quality and original video registration are unchanged.

`prepare.py` retains exact original crops/scales and writes same-canvas guides, prompts and API graphs. `run_generation.py` / `stage_video.py` preserve sampled video latents, release large models only with an empty queue, decode the original latent with tiled VAE, and verify all 124 decoded RGB frames against FFV1. `segment.py` verifies pinned local BiRefNet/code/dependency hashes before inferring soft alpha at unchanged source coordinates. No source motion synthesis, per-frame scaling, painted anatomy repair or interpolation is allowed.

Every generation, model release, matting and Godot rendering driver must be held under `tools/creature_animation_lock.py gpu` until its process finishes. `drive_stages.py` can acquire that context per bounded stage itself. Unique service input/output prefix: `saltbell_caster_h3` (inputs `saltbell_caster_<take>_guide_<index>.png`). `publish_reviewed.py` owns read-current selected publication under the separate content lock. `commit_delivery.py` owns HEAD-only UID-row staging and exact indexed source-byte checks under the separate Git lock. Neither path holds GPU while waiting for another lock.

Original art, prompts/guides, latent, FFV1/MP4, histories and failed takes are permanent source/provenance. Disposable review pages, import profiles and logs live only under `.artifacts/parallel_animation_20261002/unit_veilmourn_saltbell_casters/` and are removed after acceptance/validation.
