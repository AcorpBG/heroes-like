UI mockup for a painted fantasy turn-based strategy game: the "New skirmish" setup screen where the player picks a faction, a hero and the random map's settings.

Image 1 is the current screen at 1920x1080: a painted castle landscape with an ugly dark panel of dropdown boxes over it. Keep the painted landscape, the "AURELION REACH" title and the overall lighting of image 1 exactly; remove the existing dark panel and all its dropdowns and buttons completely, and draw the new interface described below in its place.
Image 2 is the selected hero's portrait: use this exact woman (same face, hat, coat, colours) wherever the hero's large portrait is shown.
Image 3 is the game's panel frame: every panel of the new interface uses this exact style: dark warm brown lacquer, a thin antique-gold border line, and small compass-star corner ornaments with a teal gem. Buttons are dark with a thin gold outline; the chosen option of a group is filled warm gold with dark text.

No dropdown boxes anywhere: every setting is a row of small pill buttons with the chosen one highlighted in gold. Clean, readable, aligned on a grid, generous spacing, small painted icons, crisp light text (a clean sans-serif) on the dark panels, gold headings. It must look like a finished screen of the same game, not a wireframe.
