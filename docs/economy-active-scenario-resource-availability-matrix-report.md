# Economy Active Scenario Resource Availability Matrix Report

Slice: `economy-active-scenario-resource-availability-matrix-20260524-10184`

This slice strengthens the economy/town-development goal by adding an aggregate active-scenario source matrix for all nine live stockpile resources. The report is `active_scenario_resource_availability_matrix_v1` and is generated by `tests/active_scenario_resource_availability_matrix_report.py`.

Current focused evidence covers 16 active scenarios, including 15 campaign scenarios and 16 skirmish scenarios, 18 player-town cases, and 20 enemy-town cases. The split launch-surface evidence covers 17 campaign player-town cases, 18 skirmish player-town cases, 19 campaign enemy-town cases, and 20 skirmish enemy-town cases. It proves every active town development case has scenario-authored persistent source coverage for every non-gold resource used by that town's buildable development costs, including wood, ore, and the town's faction rare resource.

The current matrix covers all nine live stockpile resources:

- `gold`
- `wood`
- `ore`
- `aetherglass`
- `embergrain`
- `peatwax`
- `verdant_grafts`
- `brass_scrip`
- `memory_salt`

Resource-source evidence is content-derived from active scenario `resource_nodes` and referenced `resource_sites`:

- common-resource source coverage appears in every active scenario for `gold`, `wood`, and `ore`;
- every live resource has campaign scenario and skirmish scenario source observations;
- every rare resource has at least one active-scenario persistent source;
- every rare resource is assigned to at least one active town `development_balance.rare_resource_id`;
- every active player and enemy town case has persistent source coverage for all non-gold development-cost resources.

normal markets remain common-resource only. The matrix records `rare_resource_buying_enabled: false`, preserving the existing policy that rare resources are sourced from authored map economy fronts instead of normal market exchange.
